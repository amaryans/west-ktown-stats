Sleeper fantasy football

League history

Every season's standings for a Sleeper league, with a toggle to include games against the league median.

Your league ID is the long number in your league's URL on sleeper.com (for example sleeper.com/leagues/1124831356770168832). Any year of the league works: earlier seasons are found automatically.

Records are recomputed from each week's matchups, regular season only. "vs Median" counts a win each week you score above the league median and a loss below it. Sort order is win percentage, then points for. Not affiliated with Sleeper.
